## Description

**The Grid went dark in a single night.** Six wardens still run their sectors. At the centre, the Null Engine waits. You're Kestrel, a maintenance unit with a spare core and a stubborn streak.

Run, jump, slide and shoot through seven hand-built stages drawn entirely in glowing vector lines. Beat each warden to take its weapon, then work out whose weakness it is: the weapons form one closed loop.

- 7 stages, 7 bosses, 6 mid-bosses, 7 weapons
- Conveyor belts, crushers, wind, swimming, low gravity and blocks that switch with the beat
- Boss Rush, 20 challenge rooms with medals, a Daily run, an Endless run and Practice
- Race a ghost of your best run
- Build levels in the editor and share them with a code or a link
- Keyboard, gamepad and touch; fully remappable
- Assist modes, slower game speeds, high contrast, large text and reduced flashing

Free, open source (MIT), about 600 KB, no tracking, works offline once loaded.

## Controls
Arrows / WASD to move · Z or Space to jump · X to fire (hold to charge) · Down + Jump to slide · Q / E to switch weapons · Enter to pause. Gamepads and touch work too. Press O for settings.
